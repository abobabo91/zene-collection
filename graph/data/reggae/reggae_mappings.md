# Reggae Mappings

## Alias normalization

- `Bob Marley: Bob Marley s, Bob Marley and The Wailers, Bob Marley & The Wailers`

`Bob Marley s` is not a typo to leave alone: the folder is `Bob Marley_s Greatest Hits`,
the underscore stands in for the apostrophe, and stripping the ` greatest hits` suffix
leaves a trailing `s`. Four songs sat under that name instead of Bob Marley's.
