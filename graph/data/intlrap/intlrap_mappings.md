# International Rap Mappings

Non-US rap: `_rap/_other/` — German, UK grime, Romanian, French.

## Alias normalization

Folders named `Artist - Album` rather than just `Artist`. `folder_artist` only splits on
` - ` when the right-hand side looks like an album word (`vol`, `mixtape`, `greatest`, …),
and a plain album title does not, so the whole string became the artist.

- `Kollegah: Kollegah - King`
- `Lady Leshurr: Lady Leshurr - Lil Bit Of Lesh`
- `Salah Edin: Salah Edin - W.O. II`
- `Azad: Azad - Diskografie - Alben + Singles`
- `Samy Deluxe: Samy Deluxe - Schwarzweiss - Hip-Hop`
- `Nessbeal: Nessbeal - Gunshot`
- `Skepta: Skepta - Konnichiwa ~~`
- `Lowkey: Soundtrack to the Struggle - Lowkey`
- `Speech Debelle: Speech Debelle - Speech Therapy`
- `Kool Savas: Kool Savas Azad - One`
- `Bushido: Bushido Sido - 23`
- `RAF Camora: RAF CAMORA`

## Groups

Format is `Group Name: member 1, member 2` — the right-hand side is the line-up, not a list
of alternative spellings. Alternative spellings go in the section above.

- `Bonez MC & RAF Camora: Bonez MC, RAF Camora`
